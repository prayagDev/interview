1. Pipe |

    A pipe takes the output of one command and gives it as input to another command.

    Example:

        ls | grep ".txt"

        Think of the flow:

            ls
            ↓
            Output: all files
            ↓
            grep ".txt"
            ↓
            Shows matching files

    Another example:

        cat file.txt | grep "ERROR"

        Meaning:

        cat file.txt reads the file.
        Its output is passed to grep.
        grep shows lines containing ERROR.



The mindset 🧠
Command 1 | Command 2 | Command 3

Basically:

Take the result from the left and keep passing it to the next command.
